The key PDFs are UPSC-issued tables mirrored by ForumIAS and IASbaba, rather than provisional coaching keys. Their SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `src/data/cse-paper-archive.json`. The 2021 CSAT Series B final key accepts **C or D for question 9**. Seven questions across the eight papers are dropped. Paper covers and footers were checked to avoid applying a different booklet series.

Original question and key PDFs remain linked for comparison. `src/data/cse-text-papers-bank.json` contains the full question text, four choices and explanations in the selected booklet order, including 108 passage-based questions and 29 semantic tables. Powers, fractions and symbols are restored from the scans; the three image-only 2021 CSAT tables are transcribed. The import manifest in `CSE-TEXT-PAPERS-IMPORT.json` records paper hashes, booklet associations and reference-question rotations.

UPSC keys contain answers, not official explanations. GS explanations reuse the supplied question bank when both the question and the correct option’s wording match; missing or incompatible material is replaced with editorial explanations. All CSAT solutions are editorial, with working for quantitative questions and text-based reasoning for comprehension. The editorial notes are retained in `scripts/data/cse-*-notes.txt`. The canonical JSON bank is the runtime source; update it and its manifest hash when changing text. Provisional coaching answers do not determine scoring. Ambiguous wording and differences from the final accepted answer are explained explicitly, including the 2021 CSAT multiple-answer item. Historical current-affairs and legal questions use the examination year’s context.

Practice reports exclude dropped questions and scale the remaining items to 200: `marks per scored item = 200 / (questions − dropped)`; `score = (correct − incorrect / 3) × marks per scored item`. This is explicitly labelled a practice score, not an official UPSC result. The CSAT practice benchmark is 66/200 (33%). Both accepted choices at a multiple-answer item score equally.

Practice permits checking an answer once, then locks it and reveals the explanation beside the question. Tests reveal answers and explanations only after submission. The study view includes every question, choice and explanation and searches their text, including reading passages. Timed tests have a fixed two-hour deadline that continues while away. An attempt snapshots the booklet key and its source hash, preserving the grading used at the time of the attempt.

Archive answer sheets are stored separately under `upsc-cse-paper-archive:v1:<account-id-or-guest>` in this browser. They do not alter existing native PYQ sessions or cloud records. The page explains this and provides a JSON backup download. Browser-storage failures show a notice and retain the current visit in memory.

Validation: `tests/paper-archive.test.ts` covers all eight booklet associations, 713 scored items, dropped and multiple accepted answers, one-third penalties, deadlines, saved key snapshots and malformed progress. `tests/paper-text.test.ts` checks all 720 questions against those keys, complete choices and explanations, shared passages, transcribed tables, booklet rotations and notation. `tests/paper-archive-ui-check.mjs` exercises all eight readers, answer locking, explanation search, test submission, previous saved progress and final-key exceptions. The existing PYQ UI suite checks the original 2025 practice/test workflow.
