2012-2|Polity & Governance|Parliamentary motions|An adjournment motion draws the Lok Sabha's attention to a definite, urgent matter of public importance by interrupting ordinary business. It is not an ordinary debate request or a device to change ministers directly. Its exceptional procedure explains the urgency requirement.
2012-3|Environment & Ecology|Biodiversity regulation|The National Biodiversity Authority advises government and regulates access and intellectual-property applications involving Indian biological resources. It does not directly supervise all research on genetically modified crops. Statements 1 and 3 describe its biodiversity-access role; distinguish that from biosafety regulation.
2012-5|Environment & Ecology|National Water Mission|Recycling urban wastewater and appropriate desalination address efficient water supply. Interlinking every river or reimbursing farmers is not a stated mission measure. The mission emphasises conservation, equitable distribution and water-use efficiency, rather than a universal engineering prescription.
2012-8|Polity & Governance|Consumer protection|The historical law provided district, state and national redressal bodies and allowed complaints subject to jurisdiction and prescribed fees. It did not make every complaint universally fee-free. Read the question under the law applicable in 2012; the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 later changed the framework.
2012-9|Polity & Governance|Speaker of Lok Sabha|The Speaker is elected from the House's members, continues after dissolution until the next House's first meeting, and resigns by writing to the Deputy Speaker. The office is not held at the President's pleasure. Only the statement correctly specifying the resignation recipient is accepted here.
2012-11|Environment & Ecology|Biopesticides|Bacillus thuringiensis supplies insecticidal proteins; Beauveria bassiana is an insect-pathogenic fungus; neem has pesticidal compounds. All three can serve as biological pest-control resources. Distinguish living microbial agents from plant-derived compounds while recognising their common pest-management application.
2012-13|Science & Technology|Water treatment|Ultraviolet treatment inactivates microorganisms when water receives an adequate dose. It does not physically remove turbidity or reliably eliminate odour. Clear water helps UV penetration; disinfection and removal of dissolved or suspended contaminants are separate treatment functions.
2012-20|Science & Technology|Magnetosphere|Charged particles are deflected by Earth's magnetic field, with some channelled toward polar regions. The ozone layer principally absorbs ultraviolet radiation rather than reflecting charged particles. Upper-atmosphere moisture is not the protective magnetic mechanism. A identifies the relevant shield.
2012-25|History & Culture|Congress ministries in 1939|Congress ministries resigned after Britain committed India to the Second World War without consulting Indian representatives. The three stated alternatives do not give that principal reason, so D is accepted. Keep the chronology distinct from the later Quit India movement of 1942.
2012-26|Polity & Governance|ASHA functions|ASHA workers mobilise households, give health information and help women access antenatal care, institutional delivery and immunisation. They are not designated to conduct deliveries as their ordinary professional role. Community linkage and counselling differ from clinical duties of trained nurses or midwives.
2012-27|History & Culture|Government of India Act 1919|The 1919 reforms introduced provincial dyarchy and transferred selected responsibilities, marking a step toward provincial decentralisation. Separate Muslim electorates had already appeared under the 1909 reforms. A reform can expand an earlier arrangement without having originated it.
2012-28|History & Culture|National Social Conference|The National Social Conference provided a forum for social reform alongside Congress's political work, rather than making social reform the Congress's main deliberative agenda. B explains the institutional separation. Associations between leading reformers alone do not express the reason as precisely.
2012-30|Polity & Governance|Special powers of Rajya Sabha|Under Article 249 Rajya Sabha can authorise Parliament to legislate on a State List matter in the national interest. Under Article 312 it can enable creation of additional All India Services. These special federal functions do not make its powers identical to Lok Sabha's in every field.
2012-31|Polity & Governance|National Rural Livelihood Mission|The mission promotes collective institutions of the rural poor, especially self-help groups, and supports skills and livelihoods. It is not simply a direct universal employment guarantee or farm subsidy. Its institution-building approach distinguishes it from MGNREGA's statutory work guarantee.
2012-33|Economy|Thirteenth Finance Commission|The accepted recommendations concern a roadmap for GST and grants supporting local bodies. A national employment programme is not the commission's defining recommendation in this question. Finance Commissions principally address fiscal distribution and grants, rather than operating employment schemes.
2012-34|Economy|National Investment and Manufacturing Zones|The manufacturing policy envisaged facilitation such as coordinated clearances and technology support through the proposed Technology Acquisition and Development Fund. The listed facilitation measures belong to that policy package. Do not confuse the zones' policy provisions with automatic exemptions from every law.
2012-35|Polity & Governance|Parliamentary control over finance|Parliament scrutinises finance through the budget and demands for grants, appropriation and finance legislation, and financial committees. The listed parliamentary mechanisms are relevant; a Parliamentary Budget Office was not an established Indian institution in 2012. Examine actual institutions rather than desirable proposals.
2012-36|History & Culture|Communal Award and Poona Pact|Gandhi's 1932 fast opposed separate electorates for the Depressed Classes announced in Ramsay MacDonald's Communal Award. The resulting Poona Pact substituted reserved seats within joint electorates. C identifies the immediate trigger, rather than generic Congress-League disagreement or the conferences alone.
2012-37|History & Culture|Ryotwari settlement|Ryotwari settlement dealt directly with individual cultivators, using surveys and pattas to define landholding and revenue obligations. Its direct state-cultivator relationship contrasts with zamindari's intermediary structure. The accepted statements describe that administrative arrangement, not security from every possible eviction or revenue burden.
2012-38|Geography|Demographic transition|High birth and death rates come first; declining mortality with relatively high births follows; low birth and death rates characterise the later stage. Thus the supplied statements must be ordered 2–3–1. Population growth accelerates during the middle transition before fertility catches up.
2012-41|Environment & Ecology|Sea buckthorn|Sea buckthorn has ecological value in fragile mountain landscapes and nutritional uses, supporting soil-conservation and food-related propositions. Claims about it as a substantial timber source or the specified biodiesel feedstock are not accepted. A plant's multiple uses do not make every proposed commercial use equally established.
2012-44|Agriculture|Legumes and crop uses|UPSC accepts cowpea and green gram for the particular combination of uses stated. Both can support food, fodder and soil improvement through legume cultivation. Pigeon pea has valuable uses too, but it is not included in the final accepted combination; avoid turning this paper-specific selection into a denial of its agronomic value.
2012-50|Environment & Ecology|Ocean acidification|Dissolved CO2 lowers seawater pH and reduces carbonate availability, affecting organisms that build calcium-carbonate shells or skeletons. The biological effects in statements 1–3 follow. Cloud seeding does not arise as the relevant direct consequence of this carbonate-chemistry process.
2012-52|Polity & Governance|Janani Suraksha Yojana|The scheme promotes institutional delivery and supports eligible pregnant women through maternity-related cash assistance and health-system linkage. It is not a general wage-compensation programme covering every period of pregnancy. Evaluate the scheme's stated beneficiary and service objectives as applicable in 2012.
2012-53|Polity & Governance|Appointment of Prime Minister|A Prime Minister need not be a Lok Sabha member at appointment and may belong to either House. A minister who is not a member of either House must secure membership within six months under Article 75(5). Appointment and continued constitutional eligibility are separate questions.
2012-55|Economy|Employees State Insurance|ESI can cover employees in the listed categories of establishments when the statutory notification, establishment size and wage conditions apply. Coverage is not limited to government staff. The accepted broad categories do not imply that every worker in every establishment is automatically insured.
2012-57|Polity & Governance|Joint sitting of Parliament|Article 108 provides a joint sitting to resolve specified deadlocks over ordinary bills. It does not apply to Money Bills or constitutional amendment bills. Remember the bill category first; a disagreement between Houses alone cannot extend the procedure to those exceptions.
2012-58|Polity & Governance|District Rural Development Agencies|DRDAs coordinate and monitor rural development and anti-poverty implementation, including attention to disadvantaged groups. They are not themselves Panchayati Raj elected institutions or primarily research organisations. Their administrative programme role explains the accepted statements 3 and 4.
2012-72|History & Culture|Bhumisparsha mudra|Bhumisparsha is the earth-touching gesture: the Buddha calls the earth to witness his achievement and steadfastness against Mara's temptations. B expresses that symbolism. It is not a request that Earth physically guard him, nor simply a general statement about bodily mortality.
2012-74|History & Culture|Brahmo Samaj|Brahmo reform emphasised monotheism and opposed practices such as idol worship. It did not retain the Vedas as an infallible final authority in the proposition given. The movement's changing theological development should not be collapsed into unqualified scriptural orthodoxy.
2012-79|Economy|Currency value|UPSC accepts the economic factors in statements 2 and 3. The World Bank does not simply determine national currency values. Exchange values depend on demand, supply and policy conditions; the paper's exclusion of another broad factor must not be read as proof that productive capacity can never influence a currency.
2012-83|History & Culture|Government of India Act 1935|The 1935 Act distributed legislative subjects among Federal, Provincial and Concurrent Lists. That three-list framework is the relevant innovation here. Differentiate provincial autonomy under this Act from provincial dyarchy under the 1919 reforms.
2012-86|Science & Technology|Rare earth elements|Rare earths have important technological uses, and deposits occur in many countries, including India. Their name does not mean occurrence is confined to four countries. Production concentration and geological distribution are different; the accepted statements distinguish technological value from the exaggerated scarcity claim.
2012-91|Environment & Ecology|Animal pollination|Bees, birds and bats can all pollinate suitable plants. Floral shape, scent and timing reflect different pollinator adaptations. Pollination is not confined to insects, even though insects account for many familiar examples.
2012-92|Environment & Ecology|Threatened species|Use the conservation assessment and species combination expected for the 2012 paper. Threat categories can change after reassessment and differ across populations and jurisdictions. The official selection is retained as a historical exam answer, rather than presented as a current status list.
2012-93|Science & Technology|Capillarity|Surface tension with adhesion and cohesion explains water movement in narrow spaces such as pores or absorbent materials. Drinking through a straw is chiefly driven by a pressure difference. Water ascent in trees also involves transpiration pull and cohesion-tension; do not attribute its entire height to capillarity alone.
2012-95|Environment & Ecology|Oryx and chiru|Oryx are adapted to hot arid settings, while chiru inhabit cold high-altitude regions of the Tibetan Plateau. Chiru are targeted for their fine underfur, not musk, and the asserted India-only distributions are incorrect. A is the valid ecological distinction.
2012-96|Environment & Ecology|Threats to biodiversity|Habitat destruction and fragmentation, overexploitation and invasive species can reduce biodiversity. Vegetarian dietary choice is not, by itself, the listed direct threat. Analyse the ecological process rather than assuming every human behaviour has the same biodiversity effect.
2012-97|Environment & Ecology|Indian fauna in historical context|The accepted combination reflects wildlife occurrence in 2012, when the cheetah was locally extinct in India. The later cheetah reintroduction changes the present-day context. Preserve that date distinction when revising historical fauna questions.
2012-98|Agriculture|Soil carbon and cultivation|The official selection identifies zero tillage for retaining carbon in agricultural soil by limiting disturbance and decomposition of soil organic matter. Other practices can have indirect sustainability benefits, but they are not in the accepted combination for this wording. Distinguish a direct soil-carbon mechanism from a broad environmental benefit.
