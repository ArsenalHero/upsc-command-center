2013-1|Polity & Governance|Public Accounts Committee|The PAC has 22 members: 15 from Lok Sabha and seven from Rajya Sabha. Ministers are not members. It examines public accounts and audit findings; membership of both Houses does not convert it into a body chaired necessarily by a ruling-party minister.
2013-8|History & Culture|Samkhya philosophy|Samkhya recognises a distinction between consciousness and material nature and seeks liberation through discriminating knowledge. It accepts the rebirth framework in the proposition given. Do not equate every orthodox philosophical school with identical positions on a creator deity.
2013-19|Polity & Governance|Planning institutions and local government|The National Development Council was a separate planning institution, not merely an organ of the Planning Commission. Economic and social planning is a Concurrent List subject, while Article 243G addresses Panchayat planning and implementation subject to state law. These are related but distinct arrangements.
2013-20|Polity & Governance|Rajya Sabha and elections|The Deputy Chairman must be a Rajya Sabha member. Nominated members participate in the Vice-President's electoral college, but not the President's. Separate the constitutional composition of the two electoral colleges before evaluating a statement about nominated members.
2013-21|Polity & Governance|National Legal Services Authority|NALSA formulates legal-services policies and supports access to justice for eligible groups through the legal-services system. Both propositions describing guidance and service support are accepted. Legal aid is linked to constitutional access to justice and the Legal Services Authorities Act, not only courtroom fee reimbursement.
2013-24|Economy|Liquidity of money and deposits|Currency is most immediately spendable, followed by demand deposits and then less immediately accessible savings/time deposits in the ordering supplied. Liquidity means ease of use or conversion without significant loss. Higher interest or a longer maturity does not mean greater liquidity.
2013-27|Environment & Ecology|Industrial water use|Thermal power generation has large water requirements, especially for cooling, and is the accepted industrial category in the historical comparison. Water withdrawal and actual consumption are different measures; do not apply the ranking blindly to every location or later technology.
2013-32|Economy|Effects of inflation|Inflation can change purchasing power, distribution and savings incentives through all three stated channels. Effects depend on whether incomes, prices and financial returns adjust. Avoid assuming every group experiences inflation identically or that every nominal increase is a real gain.
2013-39|Science & Technology|Optical phenomena|The accepted combination is 1,2,4, involving changes in light propagation or perception. Refraction through a variable atmosphere explains twinkling and related apparent-position effects; reddening chiefly involves scattering. The paper uses “optical illusion” broadly, so distinguish the physical mechanisms when revising.
2013-46|Science & Technology|Higgs boson|The Higgs field is associated with the masses of fundamental particles in the Standard Model; the boson is its excitation. It is not a demonstrated route to teleportation or a new nuclear fuel. Its discovery confirms a theoretical mechanism rather than creating those unrelated technologies.
2013-47|Environment & Ecology|Mycorrhiza|Fungal associations with roots can improve nutrient and water access and, in suitable conditions, assist tolerance of salinity or disease stress. All three benefits are accepted. They are possible ecological advantages, not a guarantee that every fungal association benefits every host under every condition.
2013-48|Polity & Governance|National Development Council composition|The historical NDC included the Prime Minister, relevant Union-level members and state leadership in the stated combination. Membership of the Finance Commission is not the defining institutional category. Its coordination role must be distinguished from the constitutional Finance Commission's separate fiscal mandate.
2013-51|Polity & Governance|Money Bills|Rajya Sabha may recommend changes to a Money Bill, but Lok Sabha may accept or reject them. A joint sitting is not available for this category. The fourteen-day return mechanism reflects the Lok Sabha's constitutional predominance in money legislation.
2013-52|Polity & Governance|Governor's office|A Governor holds office during the President's pleasure and may serve more than one state. Removal does not use impeachment, and appointment of High Court judges is a presidential function. Do not transfer the President's removal procedure or judicial appointment power to the Governor.
2013-53|Geography|Mountain ranges and continents|The Atlas Mountains are in north-western Africa. The other pairings incorrectly assign their mountain ranges to continents. Location questions are best solved by mapping each range independently, rather than inferring continent from the names' linguistic resemblance.
2013-55|Science & Technology|Gene transfer|The near-universal genetic code permits genes to be transferred across the listed biological categories using biotechnology. Cross-species transfer is not restricted to natural breeding compatibility. Expression still depends on appropriate regulation and host conditions; transfer alone does not guarantee a useful phenotype.
2013-57|Environment & Ecology|Fauna of India|Star tortoise, monitor lizard and pygmy hog occur in India; spider monkeys are native to the Americas. The accepted combination excludes the spider monkey. Similar common names or primate status do not imply an Indian natural distribution.
2013-59|History & Culture|Constituent Assembly|Members were predominantly elected indirectly by provincial legislative assemblies under the Cabinet Mission arrangement, with representation from princely states through a different route. It was not elected by direct universal adult franchise. Distinguish its formation from later parliamentary elections.
2013-60|Science & Technology|Mammals|Cow and lion are mammals. Seahorse is a fish even though the male carries developing offspring in a brood pouch. An unusual reproductive feature does not change its taxonomic class; mammalian traits include mammary glands and the relevant anatomical lineage.
2013-61|Polity & Governance|Constitutional amendment|An Article 368 amendment may originate in either House. Specified federal amendments require ratification by at least half the states, not all states. A joint sitting cannot resolve disagreement over an amendment. The proposed exclusive-House and all-state requirements are therefore incorrect.
2013-63|Agriculture|Sugar industry by-products|Bagasse can be used as fuel and molasses as a feedstock for ethanol production. The supplied chemical-fertilizer proposition is not part of the accepted combination. Different by-products have different industrial uses; do not substitute press mud or another residue for the material named.
2013-67|Geography|North-east landscape pairings|Nokrek is in the Garo Hills, the correctly matched pair. Loktak is in Manipur rather than the Barail Range, and Namdapha is associated with the Patkai region rather than the Dafla Hills. Administrative state and physical range should both be checked.
2013-69|Polity & Governance|Status of institutions|The Planning Commission and NDC were created by executive arrangements; Zonal Councils derive from statute. None of these bodies is established directly by the Constitution in the way the Election Commission or Finance Commission is. Executive, statutory and constitutional are distinct classifications.
2013-73|Environment & Ecology|Ecosystem productivity|The accepted order of productivity per unit area is mangroves, grasslands, lakes and oceans in the stated comparison. A large ecosystem may have high total production but low production per unit area. Always identify which productivity measure the question asks about.
2013-74|Agriculture|Contour bunding|Contour bunds slow runoff on sloping land and help conserve soil and water. The relevant terrain condition is absent from the supplied descriptions, giving the accepted “none” option. Bunding is not a universal remedy for every flat, waterlogged or sandy setting.
2013-77|Science & Technology|Aflatoxins|Aflatoxins are toxic compounds produced by certain moulds, particularly Aspergillus species, that can contaminate stored food. They are not naturally the product of viruses or worms. Moisture control and appropriate storage reduce conditions favouring fungal growth.
2013-80|Environment & Ecology|Acid rain chemistry|The official selection is D. Acid deposition is associated with sulfur dioxide and nitrogen oxides such as NO and NO2, which form acids after atmospheric reactions. If the option says “nitrous oxide”, that wording is chemically imprecise: N2O is not the principal nitrogen-oxide precursor of acid rain. Keep the official key and the scientific distinction together.
2013-83|Science & Technology|Nitrogen fixation|Nostoc is a cyanobacterium capable of nitrogen fixation in appropriate cells/conditions. Agaricus is a fungus and Spirogyra an alga, not the listed nitrogen-fixing cyanobacterium. Only 2 is accepted; photosynthesis alone does not imply atmospheric nitrogen fixation.
2013-88|Polity & Governance|Directive Principles|Article 37 says Directive Principles are fundamental in governance, while remaining non-justiciable. They guide legislation and public policy rather than creating directly enforceable court remedies by themselves. Non-justiciability does not make them constitutionally unimportant.
2013-99|Environment & Ecology|Photochemical smog|Sunlight-driven reactions involving nitrogen oxides and hydrocarbons produce secondary pollutants such as ozone and PAN. The mechanism distinguishes photochemical smog from simple smoke accumulation or sulfurous industrial fog. Ground-level ozone is harmful even though stratospheric ozone shields ultraviolet radiation.
2013-100|Science & Technology|Muscle function and minerals|The final key accepts all listed minerals as relevant to the broad maintenance/function proposition. Calcium participates in contraction signalling and sodium in excitation; iron supports oxygen delivery. Do not describe iron as the same direct contractile signal as calcium merely because all are accepted.
