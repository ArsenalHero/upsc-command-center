2014-1|History & Culture|Medieval village administration|Mahattara and Pattakila were village-level functionaries, so B is correct. Mahattaras were influential village elders, while Pattakilas were associated with village headmanship and revenue administration. The terms belong to local administration rather than the army, Vedic ritual specialisation or craft guilds.
2014-4|Environment & Ecology|Industrial emissions|All four gases can arise from steel production, making D correct. Sulphur in fuels and ores produces sulphur oxides; high-temperature combustion produces nitrogen oxides; incomplete combustion produces carbon monoxide; combustion and reduction processes release carbon dioxide. Carbon dioxide is a greenhouse gas even though its effects differ from those of the local air pollutants.
2014-6|Environment & Ecology|Community conservation|The Gond and Korku communities are associated with the month-long Hari Jiroti tradition of planting fruit-bearing trees. B is correct. Such practices combine cultural observance with regeneration of vegetation and community access to useful trees; the other tribal pairs do not identify the tradition asked about.
2014-7|Economy|Indirect taxation before GST|In the 2014 tax system, sales tax on an ordinary retail purchase was imposed and collected by the State Government, D. Distinguish this from central excise on manufacture and the separate rules for inter-State sales. GST subsequently changed the taxation framework, so this historical question must be answered in its examination-year context.
2014-14|International Relations|Arab Spring|The Arab Spring began in Tunisia in late 2010 and spread to countries including Egypt, Libya and Syria. D identifies the starting point; Egypt was a prominent subsequent centre of protest. The question tests the origin of the regional movement rather than the country receiving the most later news coverage.
2014-15|International Relations|Arctic Council|Denmark, Russia and the United States are Arctic Council member States: 1, 3 and 5, D. Denmark participates through the Kingdom of Denmark, including Greenland and the Faroe Islands. Japan and the United Kingdom are observers, not member States. Membership and observer status are different institutional categories.
2014-16|Geography|Regions in the news|Only Chechnya–Russian Federation is correctly matched, A. Darfur is in Sudan, while the Swat Valley is in Pakistan. Locate the region within its country before relating it to the conflict reported in the news; neither Mali nor Iraq fits the respective pair.
2014-17|Science & Technology|Agni missile series|Only statement 1 is correct, A. Agni-IV is a surface-to-surface ballistic missile using solid propellant, with an approximately 4,000 km design range. Liquid propulsion and a 7,500 km range are incorrect descriptions of this missile. Similar names within a missile family do not imply identical range or propulsion.
2014-18|Science & Technology|Unconventional natural gas|Neither statement is correct, D. Shale gas is principally methane, not a mixture consisting only of propane and butane. Indian shale formations with gas potential had already been identified by 2014. Coalbed methane and shale gas differ primarily in their geological reservoirs; identification of a resource is distinct from commercial production.
2014-28|History & Culture|Regional performing arts|Garba belongs to Gujarat and Yakshagana to Karnataka, so 1 and 3 are correct, C. Mohiniattam is a classical dance of Kerala, not Odisha. Odisha is especially associated with Odissi. Matching each form with its region prevents confusion between geographically close but distinct traditions.
2014-30|History & Culture|Bhakti traditions|Both statements are incorrect, D. Bijak preserves teachings associated with Kabir. Pushti Marg, the path of divine grace, was founded by Vallabhacharya and is associated with Shuddhadvaita. Dadu Dayal and Madhvacharya belong to different devotional and philosophical traditions.
2014-32|History & Culture|Queen Victoria's Proclamation|Statements 1 and 2 are correct, A. The 1858 proclamation announced Crown rule and a policy of respecting princely treaties and disclaiming territorial expansion. It did not regulate the East India Company's Indian trade: the Company's commercial privileges had already ended before 1858. Separate the transfer of political authority from earlier changes to trading rights.
2014-44|Environment & Ecology|Uses of neem|UPSC accepts 1 and 3 only, C. Neem oil contains compounds with insecticidal activity and has pharmaceutical applications. Statement 2 joins biofuel manufacture to the specific claim about hospital detergents; the full combined claim was not accepted in the final key. Do not infer that every product described as neem-based establishes both parts of that statement.
2014-48|Science & Technology|Planetary exploration|Statements 2 and 3 are correct, B. MESSENGER studied Mercury, and Voyager 1 and 2 explored the outer solar system. Cassini–Huygens studied Saturn and its moons, including Titan, rather than Venus. The target body is the decisive distinction between these missions.
2014-51|Polity & Governance|Supreme Court jurisdiction|Article 131 gives the Supreme Court original jurisdiction over specified disputes between the Union and States or between States, C. Original jurisdiction means the dispute begins in that court. Appellate jurisdiction reviews lower-court decisions; advisory jurisdiction concerns presidential references; writ jurisdiction protects rights through specified remedies.
2014-52|Science & Technology|Transgenic crop techniques|The final key accepts cytoplasmic male sterility and gene silencing, 2 and 3, B. Genetic engineering can manipulate male sterility for hybrid production and silence selected genes. Budding and grafting are ordinary vegetative propagation methods and do not themselves introduce a transgene. Male sterility also occurs naturally, so not every male-sterile crop is necessarily transgenic.
2014-55|Polity & Governance|Number of Supreme Court judges|Parliament determines the sanctioned strength through legislation, B, under Article 124. This is distinct from the President's appointment of individual judges. The Chief Justice and Law Commission may make recommendations, but recommendations do not themselves alter the statutory number of judges.
2014-57|Geography|Historical national highway numbering|None of the pairs is correct, D, under the old highway numbering used in this question. Old NH4 linked Mumbai and Chennai; NH6 ran from the Surat region to Kolkata; NH15 ran from Pathankot towards Samakhiali through western India. Later renumbering should not be substituted for the examination's historical labels.
2014-60|Environment & Ecology|Aquatic food chains|Diatoms → crustaceans → herrings is the correct sequence, A. Photosynthetic diatoms act as primary producers, small crustaceans consume plankton, and herrings feed on zooplankton. A food chain follows transfer of energy from producer to consumers, not merely the size or abundance of organisms.
2014-63|Geography|Indian island channels|The Ten Degree Channel separates the Andaman group from the Nicobar group, A, near 10° north latitude. The channel is within the Andaman and Nicobar archipelago. The other pairs involve different oceanic passages and do not match this latitude-based name.
2014-66|Environment & Ecology|Global Environment Facility|The GEF serves as a financial mechanism for environmental conventions including the Convention on Biological Diversity and UNFCCC, A. It finances projects through partner agencies rather than functioning as a global scientific research institute. It is not an OECD agency; hence neither the research description nor the combined A-and-B choice is correct.
2014-68|History & Culture|Classical language recognition|Kannada and Telugu had classical-language status by the 2014 examination, C. Both were recognised in 2008. Gujarati had not received that designation. Recognition dates matter in historical current-affairs questions; later additions to the classical-language list do not change the answer for 2014.
2014-69|Geography|Protected areas and peaks|Dampa Tiger Reserve is in Mizoram and Saramati Peak is in Nagaland, so 1 and 3 are correct, C. Gumti Wildlife Sanctuary is in Tripura, not Sikkim. The question combines a reserve, sanctuary and mountain; identify the State separately for each feature.
2014-71|International Relations|Early BRICS summits|Only statement 2 is correct, B, in the 2014 context. The first BRIC leaders' summit was in Yekaterinburg, Russia, in 2009; South Africa subsequently joined the original four countries, producing BRICS. Rio de Janeiro was not the first summit venue. Later expansion of the grouping must not be read into this historical membership question.
2014-77|Environment & Ecology|Hibernation|Hibernation or comparable seasonal torpor occurs in species of bats, bears and rodents, C. It reduces energy expenditure when food availability or temperatures are unfavourable. The statement concerns occurrence within each animal group, not a claim that every species or every individual in those groups hibernates.
2014-79|Environment & Ecology|Carbon cycle|Volcanic activity, respiration and decomposition can release carbon dioxide, so 1, 2 and 4 are correct, C. Photosynthesis takes up carbon dioxide to build organic matter. Organisms may both photosynthesise and respire, but the named process of photosynthesis is a carbon sink rather than a release process.
2014-82|Environment & Ecology|Soil erosion|The final key accepts deforestation only, B. Removing vegetation exposes soil and reduces root binding, increasing erosion. Terracing generally checks runoff and erosion on slopes. Tropical climate alone does not imply erosion: vegetation cover, rainfall intensity, slope and management determine whether soil is lost.
2014-84|History & Culture|Panchayatana temples|Panchayatana is a temple layout with a central shrine and four subsidiary shrines, C. The five-shrine arrangement explains the name. It refers to architecture in this question, rather than a village assembly or an administrative post; similar Sanskrit roots can occur in different institutional contexts.
2014-87|History & Culture|Six orthodox philosophical systems|Lokayata and Kapalika do not form a pair among the six orthodox darshanas, C. The six are Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Sankhya, Yoga, Mimamsa and Vedanta. Lokayata is associated with materialism, while Kapalika denotes a Shaiva tradition; neither completes the conventional list asked about.
2014-90|Environment & Ecology|Biosphere reserves|Biosphere reserves explicitly combine biodiversity conservation with sustainable livelihoods and traditional human use, A. Their core, buffer and transition zones permit different degrees of protection and activity. Botanical gardens mainly conserve plants ex situ; national parks and wildlife sanctuaries have different statutory purposes and restrictions.
2014-92|Geography|Southeast Asian latitudes|From south to north the order is Jakarta, Singapore, Bangkok, Hanoi: 3–4–1–2, C. Jakarta lies south of the equator; Singapore is just north of it; Bangkok and Hanoi are progressively farther north. Latitude, rather than east–west position or travel distance, determines the required sequence.
2014-95|Polity & Governance|International peace in the Constitution|Article 51 places promotion of international peace and security among the Directive Principles of State Policy, B. It also concerns just relations, respect for international law and arbitration. Fundamental Duties are in Article 51A, which is a separate provision despite the similar number.
2014-97|Economy|National planning institutions in 2014|UPSC accepts the National Development Council and Parliament, 2 and 5, C, for the national planning framework then in force. The NDC considered plans and Parliament exercised legislative and financial oversight. Sectoral ministries also prepared and implemented programmes, but that broader everyday meaning of planning is not the institutional distinction used by the final key. The Finance Commission's principal role is fiscal transfers, not formulation of Five-Year Plans.
