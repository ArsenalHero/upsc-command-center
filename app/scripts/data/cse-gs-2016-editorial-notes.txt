2016-1|Economy|Atal Pension Yojana|Statements 1 and 3 are correct, C. APY offers defined minimum pension benefits linked to contributions, with a government guarantee, and the same minimum pension is payable to the surviving spouse after the subscriber’s death. Eligible spouses may also enrol separately in their own right. The scheme was not restricted to one enrolment per family. Individual eligibility and contributions must be distinguished from household-level benefits.
2016-2|International Relations|RCEP negotiations in 2016|RCEP was an ASEAN-centred regional economic initiative, B. The 2016 negotiations involved ASEAN and its free-trade partners, including India at that stage. India's later withdrawal from the negotiations does not alter the examination-year answer. The grouping concerns economic integration rather than the G20 or SCO.
2016-3|Environment & Ecology|Energy efficiency labels|All three product groups were covered by BEE energy-efficiency labelling programmes, D: ceiling fans, electric geysers and tubular fluorescent lamps. A star label compares energy performance, helping consumers assess operating costs. Coverage by the programme should not be confused with whether labelling was mandatory for every product category at a given time.
2016-6|International Relations|International Solar Alliance|Only statement 1 is correct, A. The ISA was launched at the 2015 Paris climate conference with an initial focus on solar-rich countries between the Tropics. It did not initially include every UN member. Subsequent expansion of eligibility does not validate the broad membership claim for the 2016 question.
2016-7|Economy|European Stability Mechanism|The European Stability Mechanism provides financial assistance to euro-area members under specified conditions, B. It is a crisis-support institution for monetary-union members rather than a global development agency or a general EU trade mechanism. Euro-area membership is narrower than European Union membership.
2016-8|Environment & Ecology|Drip irrigation|Reduced weed growth and reduced soil erosion are accepted advantages, 1 and 3, C. Delivering water near the root zone limits wetting of surrounding soil and surface runoff. Drip irrigation does not guarantee lower salinity; salt distribution and accumulation depend on water quality, drainage and management. A useful technique should not be assigned an unconditional benefit.
2016-9|Science & Technology|DigiLocker|Both statements are correct, C. DigiLocker provides digital access to documents and supports sharing them with authorised recipients, reducing dependence on physical copies. It is associated with the Digital India programme. Digital storage and electronic verification are complementary functions rather than competing descriptions.
2016-15|Environment & Ecology|State butterfly recognition|Maharashtra became the first Indian State to declare a State butterfly, selecting the Blue Mormon in 2015, D. The question asks the first State and should be answered using that historical announcement. Later declarations by other States do not change the first occurrence.
2016-26|Polity & Governance|Prorogation and dissolution|Only statement 2 is correct, B. Prorogation ends a session but does not cause a pending bill to lapse. A bill pending in Rajya Sabha that has not been passed by Lok Sabha survives Lok Sabha's dissolution. Other pending-bill categories can lapse on dissolution, so distinguish dissolution of a House from termination of a session.
2016-27|Health & Society|Global Hunger Index|All the listed dimensions enter the GHI framework, C. The methodology used around this examination combined undernourishment, child wasting, child stunting and under-five mortality. Stunting reflects chronic undernutrition, wasting acute undernutrition, and mortality its wider fatal consequences. The three listed statements need not exhaust every indicator to be correct.
2016-28|Economy|Reducing a fiscal deficit|Reducing revenue expenditure and rationalising subsidies can reduce the fiscal deficit, 1 and 3, C, other things equal. Expanding welfare expenditure raises spending, while cutting import duties can reduce revenue. A deficit is expenditure minus receipts excluding borrowing; examine the direction of each change on either side of that balance.
2016-33|Economy|Masala bonds and IFC|Both statements are correct, C. IFC is part of the World Bank Group and issued rupee-denominated offshore bonds popularly called Masala bonds. Rupee denomination places exchange-rate exposure on foreign investors rather than making the borrower's obligation dollar-denominated. The issuer's international status does not determine the currency of the bond.
2016-39|Economy|IMF Special Drawing Rights|The Chinese renminbi was added to the SDR currency basket, D, with effect from October 2016. It joined the US dollar, euro, Japanese yen and pound sterling. SDRs are an international reserve asset based on a currency basket, not a currency issued for ordinary retail use.
2016-41|Health & Society|Garima Abhiyan|Garima Abhiyan concerns the dignity and rehabilitation of manual scavengers, C. Its focus is ending an occupation rooted in caste discrimination and supporting alternative livelihoods and rights. It should not be confused with unrelated environmental, educational or generic employment campaigns.
